The Conference

About PyHo

PyHo is the annual Python conference for Ho, the Volta Region, and the wider Ghana developer community — two days of talks, workshops, sprints, and real connections.

From beginner-friendly workshops for first-time coders to deep technical sessions for experienced engineers, PyHo is where the Python community in the Volta Region comes alive. We believe great software begins with great communities.

Learn more about PyHo →
Community-driven

Organized entirely by volunteers from the Python User Group Ho. Every session is submitted by the community, reviewed by the community, and built for the community.

Open & Inclusive

PyHo actively supports diversity in tech. We partner with Django Girls Ho to bring new voices into the Python world and provide financial assistance for those who need it.

PSF-affiliated

Organized in partnership with the Python Software Foundation — the nonprofit behind Python and events like PyCon — sharing their commitment to community-first values.

What's Happening

Three Events, One Weekend

PyHo 2026 spans three distinct events across the same weekend. Come for one, stay for all.

Django Girls Ho

October 22–23, 2026

Django Girls Ho Workshop

We're proud to host the Django Girls Ho workshop as part of PyHo 2026 — a free, full-day programming workshop for women and non-binary people who want to build their first web app with Python and Django. No prior experience required.

Free to attend. Registration is via the Django Girls website. Spots are limited — apply early.
Register on djangogirls.org
Builders Lab Sprint New 2026

October 23, 2026

PyHo Builders Lab Sprint

A focused, hands-on coding sprint where developers team up to build, contribute, and ship real projects. Whether you're contributing to open-source Python libraries, hacking on community tools, or prototyping something entirely new — the Builders Lab is your space to make things.

Calling track partners & sponsors. Join us in supporting individual sprint tracks. Contact us about partnership opportunities.
Learn about the Sprint

Main Conference Day
