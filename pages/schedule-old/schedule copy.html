Pre-Conference Workshop · October 22–23

Django Girls Ho 2026

A free, two-day hands-on workshop for women and non-binary people who want to build their first web app with Python and Django. No prior experience needed.

Django Girls Ho · October 22–23, 2026 · Ho, Ghana

Build your first web app with Python & Django

Two days, dedicated coaches, one complete Django blog application. Every participant finishes with a real, deployed web app built using the official Django Girls tutorial.

📍 Ho, Volta Region 🆓 Free to attend 👩‍💻 Women & non-binary people 🐍 No experience needed
1

Day One — Welcome & Environment Setup

Wednesday, October 22, 2026
9:00 AM

Registration & Welcome

Participants arrive, collect name tags, meet coaches. Organisers introduce the two-day format and what to expect.

9:30 AM

Introduction to Programming & Python

What is programming? What is Python? Accessible intro for absolute beginners. Coaches work through core concepts in pairs with participants.

10:30 AM

Installation Party 🎉

Setting up the development environment — Python, code editor (VS Code), Git, and virtual environments. Coaches troubleshoot every laptop individually. Nobody gets left behind.

12:30 PM

Lunch Break

Catered lunch. Participants and coaches eat together — community-building starts here.

1:30 PM

Django Environment Configuration

Installing Django, creating the first project, understanding the project structure. Command-line basics for anyone who hasn't used a terminal before.

3:00 PM

How the Web Works

A practical explanation of requests, responses, servers, and browsers — the foundation that makes Django make sense.

4:00 PM

Q&A & Networking

Open discussion. Coaches share their stories. Participants preview what they'll build on Day 2.

5:00 PM

Day One Closes

Optional networking dinner for participants and coaches — details confirmed separately.

2

Day Two — Building the Django Blog App

Thursday, October 23, 2026
9:00 AM

Day Two Welcome & Recap

Quick recap of Day 1. Coaches check environments are still working. The Django Girls tutorial opens.

9:30 AM

Django Models & the Database

Creating the blog post model, running migrations, using the Django admin panel to add and manage content.

11:00 AM

Django Views & URLs

Writing views, mapping URLs, and connecting user requests to database content. The app starts to come alive.

12:30 PM

Lunch Break

Catered lunch. Participants compare progress and encourage each other through the afternoon ahead.

1:30 PM

Django Templates & Static Files

Building HTML templates, template inheritance, adding CSS — making the blog look like a real website.

2:45 PM

Forms & Post Publishing

Adding a form to create and publish blog posts from the front end. The app becomes fully functional.

3:30 PM

Deployment to the Web

Deploying the completed blog so it's live on the internet. Every participant finishes with a real, publicly accessible URL.

4:15 PM

Celebration & Certificate Ceremony 🎓

Participants share their live sites. Coaches reflect. Certificates of completion presented. Django Girls Ho 2026 closes.

Following the official Django Girls tutorial. All participants use the step-by-step guide at tutorial.djangogirls.org/en. Event details and registration at djangogirls.org/en/ho.

Builders Lab Sprint Schedule

Builders Lab Sprint · October 23, 2026 · Ho, Ghana

Build. Ship. Showcase.

From kickoff to live demo in one day. Partner sessions from Cursor Ghana and Replit Ghana are embedded into the sprint flow, giving teams hands-on access to modern developer tooling mid-build.

Register for Builders Lab

Partner Sessions

⚡

Cursor Ghana

Session at 10:30 AM. AI-assisted development workflows, code generation, and live refactoring demo — practical tooling for all sprint tracks.

Replit Ghana

Session at 2:30 PM. Cloud development environments, collaborative coding, and rapid deployment workflows — timed for the afternoon build phase.

Sprint Day Timetable · October 23, 2026

All times approximate
8:00 AM

Registration & Arrival

Badge collection, welcome pack, track assignment confirmation.

8:30 AM

Kickoff — Problem Alignment & Team Formation

Track introductions, partner briefings, team formation, and problem statement presentations. The sprint clock starts.

9:30 AM

Build Phase Begins

Active development across all five tracks — FinTech, AI/Data, Web3, HealthTech, AgriTech. Mentors available in each track room throughout.

10:30 AM

Partner Session — Cursor Ghana

AI-assisted development workflows and live demo. Participants can attend and return to their track build immediately after.

Cursor Ghana
12:00 PM

Mid-Sprint Check-in

Progress review with track mentors. Course-correction, unblocking, and team encouragement. Are you on track to demo something by 5:30?

12:30 PM

Lunch Break

Catered lunch for all participants. Sprint clock pauses. Teams rest and regroup before the afternoon push.

1:30 PM

Afternoon Build Phase

Sprint resumes. Final major development window. Mentor check-ins continue. Partners remain on the floor for guidance.

2:30 PM

Partner Session — Replit Ghana

Cloud development environments and collaborative coding. Practical integration with active sprint projects — timing chosen for mid-afternoon build energy.

Replit Ghana
3:30 PM

Final Build Window

Last major development block. Mentors shift to polish mode — helping teams reach a working demo, not add features.

4:30 PM

Demo Prep

Teams freeze development. Prepare a 3-minute demo pitch showing what was built, what problem it solves, and what's next.

5:30 PM

Internal Track Demos

Each team demos to their track mentor and partner representative. Judge scoring begins. Top projects selected for the public showcase on Oct 24.

7:30 PM

Sprint Ends · Final Submission

All prototypes submitted. Teams join the conference opening ceremony at 6:00 PM. Public showcase is scheduled for Oct 24 at 6:00 PM.

Builders Lab public showcase is on Oct 24 at 6:00 PM — live demos to the full conference audience, partner judge awards, and recognition of standout projects. All conference attendees are welcome.
