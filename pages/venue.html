Host City

Welcome to Ho

Capital of the Volta Region and a city that runs on community spirit — Ho is the perfect home for a Pythonista gathering.

Ho sits at roughly 900 m above sea level in the southeastern highlands of Ghana, surrounded by forested hills and the sweeping Volta basin that gives the region its name.

It is a city of teachers, engineers, traders, and storytellers — known for its calm pace, its university energy (University of Health and Allied Sciences is based here), and its easy distance from major Ghanaian cities without any of the Accra sprawl.

The Ewe people are the dominant cultural group; visitors find a city proud of its weaving traditions, its oral history, and its warm hospitality. Kente cloth woven in Volta is among the most prized in Ghana.

  • A central, walkable urban core — most conference hotels are within 3 km of the venue
  • Year-round warm climate; October is post-rainy season — dry, breezy, and comfortable
  • Rich Ewe cultural scene: festivals, craft markets, and local cuisine worth exploring
  • Excellent mobile data coverage across all major Ghanaian networks
  • The Wli Waterfalls — Ghana's highest — are a short day-trip away

Travel Guide

Getting to Ho

Ho is well-connected by road from Accra and other major Ghanaian cities. Here's how to plan your journey.

🚌

From Accra by Bus

VIP Jeoun, STC, and several private coach operators run direct Accra → Ho services daily. Coaches depart from Accra's Tudu / Asafo terminals and Kaneshie.

Approx. 4 – 5 hrs · GHS 40 – 80 depending on class
🚗

Self-Drive from Accra

Take the N1 east past Tema, then the N2 northeast through Sogakope or Adidome. The Ho-Accra road is paved and in good condition. Google Maps routes are reliable.

Approx. 200 km · 3.5 – 4.5 hrs (allow extra for Accra traffic)
✈️

Flying In

Fly into Kotoka International Airport (ACC) in Accra. From the airport, take a taxi or rideshare to Tudu/Kaneshie terminal, then board an Ho-bound bus. Alternatively, hire a private driver for the full journey.

Accra airport → Ho · ~4 – 5 hrs total
🔄

From Kumasi

Direct tro-tros and shared taxis run Kumasi → Ho via Kpando. Journey passes through the scenic Volta highlands — a beautiful route worth the extra time.

Approx. 5 – 6 hrs · Change at Kpando if needed
🌍

From Northern Ghana

Travel south on the N1 to Accra or take regional buses through Hohoe and Kpando to reach Ho. Internal flights to Accra are also an option from Tamale.

Tamale → Ho · ~8 hrs by road
🌐

From Neighbouring Countries

Attendees from Togo can cross at the Aflao border (close to the Volta Region) and bus north to Ho. From Benin or Nigeria, fly into Kotoka then road-connect as above.

Aflao border → Ho · ~1.5 hrs

Getting Around Ho

🛺
Taxis & Shared Taxis The most common way around town. Agree the fare before you board — short trips within Ho are typically GHS 5 – 15.
📱
Bolt & Yango Both rideshare apps operate in Ho. Useful for transparent pricing and direct venue-to-hotel routing.
🚶
On Foot Ho's central area is walkable. The market, several hotels, and key landmarks are within a comfortable 20-minute walk of each other.
🏍️
Motorbike Taxis (Okada) Fast and cheap for short hops. Helmets provided by reputable operators — always check before boarding.
